# Homepage Opening Copy Design

## Intent and goals

Replace the body of the Folheto proposal opening at `/` with the owner's approved, more specific account of why Embraso exists: useful work, exchange and discovery, a direct statement of the founders' combined technology experience, and building with people who know the problems. Keep the existing approved headline. Preserve the community and gradual digital-autonomy fronts in the corresponding explanation where needed for consistency with the opening.

The copy is an editorial adaptation inspired by Thiago's manifesto, not a quotation or text attributed to its author. Public copy remains Brazilian Portuguese; this implementation spec is in English.

## Non-goals and constraints

- Do not change `/devs/`, the shared canonical manifesto, or the six canonical principles and their names/order.
- Keep community-building and gradual digital autonomy as the two inseparable fronts; they are not additional principles. Do not imply a recruitment funnel, launched product, service, or operational community.
- The owner explicitly supplied and approved the factual claim of more than 50 years of combined technology experience, including experience at large companies. Do not generalize it into other credentials or claims.
- Do not add unapproved public copy.

## Approaches considered

For the experience detail, the alternatives were an indirect statement that leaves the team's experience implicit, or a direct statement naming the combined experience and large-company background. The owner chose the direct approach; it makes the intended credibility explicit while staying within the supplied fact. The approved copy below is the source of truth.

## Approved copy

**Heading** (already present; retain exactly):

> Mais do que código.
>
> Gente com quem construir.

**Opening:**

> Queremos usar o que sabemos para fazer coisas que melhorem a vida das pessoas. Juntos, somamos mais de 50 anos de experiência trabalhando com tecnologia, inclusive em grandes empresas. Agora, queremos colocar esse conhecimento a serviço de problemas que importam por aqui.

**Exchange and opportunity:**

> Passar horas consumindo conteúdo não é o mesmo que conversar, trocar e descobrir algo inesperado. Há gente no Brasil e fora dele com conhecimento para compartilhar. Queremos aproximar essas pessoas de quem conhece os problemas — e construir junto, não produzir código por produzir.

**Tools and autonomy:**

> As ferramentas podem ajudar a transformar uma ideia em algo que funciona. Mas construir com as pessoas e ampliar, aos poucos, a autonomia sobre o que usamos também fazem parte do caminho.

## Routes and acceptance criteria

- Change the approved opening/body copy on Folheto `/`; retain its heading. Update the related community/autonomy explanation only where necessary so it remains consistent with the approved copy and keeps both fronts explicit.
- Keep all user-facing text in the exact Brazilian Portuguese wording above. Do not paraphrase, add claims, or attribute the adaptation to Thiago.
- `/devs/` and `/manifesto/` remain unchanged; canonical manifesto wording and the six principle names/order remain untouched.
- The combined-experience claim appears as owner-approved, with no inferred expansion of its scope.
